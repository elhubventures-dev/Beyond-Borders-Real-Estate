# Business Analysis — Beyond Borders

## Industry & niche
Abuja, Nigeria real estate developer and construction firm: residential estates (houses + land plots), turnkey construction, roads/bridges messaging.

## Brand
- **Name:** Beyond Borders / Beyond Borders Ltd  
- **Tagline themes:** Smart & luxury homes; tailor-made homes  
- **Voice:** Aspirational, location-led, affordability + quality  
- **Contact:** info@beyondborders.ng · +234 704 207 0950 (WhatsApp) · +234 907 171 1222 · Suite A13D Platinum Plaza, Jahi, Abuja  

## Audience
- Home buyers seeking duplexes/terraces in Abuja corridors (Idu, Lugbe, Dakwo, Kuje, Giri)  
- Land investors (300–500+ sqm plots)  
- Clients needing construction / design services  

## Customer journey
1. Discover estate via home / projects nav  
2. Compare house & land packages and features  
3. Schedule inspection or request consultation  
4. Contact via phone/WhatsApp/email  

## Conversion goals (priority)
1. **Schedule an Inspection** form submissions  
2. **Contact / Free Consultation** form submissions  
3. Click-to-call / WhatsApp  

## Revenue model
Off-plan / estate sales (houses & lands) with outright payment promos and payment plans. Website is lead-gen, not ecommerce checkout.
